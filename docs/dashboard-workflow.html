How the SEA6 IBD Market Intelligence dashboard works

A view-only market intelligence dashboard for significant new game launches, game announcements, and industry trends across Singapore, Malaysia, the Philippines, Indonesia, Thailand, and Vietnam.

1. What This Dashboard Is

The dashboard turns downloaded market evidence, PC context, game research, and reviewed news into a compact SEA6 market brief. It covers Singapore, Malaysia, the Philippines, Indonesia, Thailand, and Vietnam, with both regional and country views.

2. What Readers Can Do

  • Start with the SEA6 overview, then switch to an individual country view.
  • Read the latest brief or open historical briefs from the archive.
  • Compare Mobile + PC/Console, Mobile-only, and PC/Console-only game groups.
  • Review Game Announcements separately from Industry Trends.
  • Use Game Tracker search, year/month filters, multi-level sorting, and CSV download.
  • Follow title-specific storefront links and article source links when checking evidence.

3. End-to-End Workflow

The workflow combines repeatable data preparation with a deliberate editorial review before anything reaches the public static view.

1Download mobile evidenceShauna downloads Sensor Tower country data and current iOS/Android ranking exports.
2Download PC evidenceShauna downloads SteamDB release data for global PC context.
3Select mobile candidatesCountry-level selection rules identify significant new mobile launches.
4Add PC contextSteamDB supplies global PC releases and activity; it is not country-level sales data.
5Match exact titlesCanonical-title matching identifies confirmed mobile-PC counterparts, with verified overrides only.
6Enrich the game layerResearch supplies translated titles, publisher, controlled genre, storefront, key details, and a comparable-game reference.
7Review news candidatesGame News Radar supplies filtered article candidates for announcements and industry trends.
8Approve editorial contextEditorial approval selects report-worthy stories and assigns country relevance.
9Generate and publishThe static dashboard and historical archive are generated and quality-checked, then GitHub Pages publishes the view-only dashboard.

4. Selection Logic

  • Mobile first: a mobile game must have prior revenue of $0 and gross revenue above the current significance threshold of US$3,000 for that report period.
  • Known existing games are excluded from new-launch selection.
  • SteamDB is global, not country-specific. PC releases can appear in SEA6 and country views as global context.
  • Mobile + PC/Console requires exact canonical-title matching or an explicit verified title override. Loose fuzzy matching is not used.
  • When mobile releases first and PC appears later, continuity notes link the later PC evidence back to the earlier mobile brief.
  • Rankings are context only and depend on the depth of the downloaded Sensor Tower chart export.
  • Sensor Tower data can lag by one to two days, so the dashboard shows its data-as-of context.

5. News Logic

  • Game Radar collects and classifies gaming articles.
  • It filters guides, consumer deals, merchandise, unrelated media, and other non-report material.
  • Game Announcements and Industry Trends are separate report sections.
  • Articles must fall inside the report period.
  • Editorial approval is required before an article appears in the final report.
  • SEA6 is the roll-up: every country-specific included article also appears in SEA6. Country tabs show common regional news plus country-specific relevance.

6. Automated vs Editorial

Automated and repeatableEditorial and judgment-led
Ingestion, thresholds, exact title matching, enrichment checks, rendering, tests, and archive generation.Article relevance, country relevance, translation and summary validation, uncertain counterpart decisions, and factual corrections.
The system applies the agreed rules consistently and records structured evidence for rendering.A person decides whether a candidate is genuinely useful and accurate for the brief.

7. Data Scope and Limits

Sensor TowerRevenue, download, and ranking context for the six SEA markets.
SteamDBGlobal PC release and activity evidence, not country-level revenue.
Game RadarArticle candidates for announcements and industry trends.

Storefront links are title-specific. Console tags require sourced confirmation. The site is static and view-only: it does not provide real-time tracking or claim universal coverage.

8. Quality Controls

  • Genres must use a sourced controlled taxonomy.
  • Game titles and storefront links are checked against the exact game.
  • Release dates and news items are checked against the report period.
  • Repeated or duplicate stories are controlled before rendering.
  • Automated tests cover the export, enrichment, lifecycle, and dashboard UI layers.
  • A final editorial review checks the report before publication.

9. Current Status

This is a proof-of-concept dashboard with a latest completed brief and a historical archive. The next report is prepared when the next reporting period has enough finalized input data; the schedule and available evidence determine when that is ready.

The public pages are generated static views. They are designed for reading and review, not for editing source data in place.
